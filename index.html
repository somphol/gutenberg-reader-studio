Field guide

How to use the studio

Read

  • Search 75,000+ Project Gutenberg books or open a curated classic.
  • Use Page turn for a 3D page curl, or Continuous scroll for a long-form flow.
  • Adjust theme, typeface, size, and line height in Aa. Everything is remembered per device.

Learn words

  • Double-click a word (or select it) to open its definition, phonetics, and audio.
  • The surrounding sentence is captured automatically as context.
  • Add to Word Bank files the word under New. Review it to move it toward Practicing and Mastered.

Focus modes

  • Bionic reading bolds word prefixes for faster visual scanning.
  • RSVP flashes one word at a time at 150–500 wpm.
  • Read aloud narrates the page sentence by sentence with a moving highlight. Pick a platform voice by accent in Aa, preview it, and set the speed; L starts or stops it.
  • Auto-scroll moves the page hands-free; adjust the speed in the floating HUD.
  • Ambient sound synthesizes rain, café, and fireplace audio in the browser.

Credits & provenance

Books come from Project Gutenberg, discovered through the public Gutendex API. Definitions and audio come from the Free Dictionary API. Text is fetched from public Gutenberg mirrors and the GITenberg archive.

This studio was built in a single agent session with OpenCode 2.2.5 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. for elapsed time and token usage.

No accounts, analytics, or cookies. Your notes and vocabulary never leave this browser unless you export them.
